MoveData provides an ability to process or ignore address information, which is easily configurable in your MoveData Settings.

### NPSP

{% embed url="https://app.arcade.software/share/qfu06KYz8MCUTvswUFAm" %}

#### Transcription

* Open Settings
* Click Fundraising & Donations
* Scroll down to Contacts
* To ignore address information, select Ignore Address
* Click Update

### NPC

{% embed url="https://app.arcade.software/share/aPnVXwO8CumScxwvt67F" %}

#### Transcription

* Open Settings
* Click General
* Scroll down to Person Accounts
* To ignore address information, select Ignore Address
* Click Update
