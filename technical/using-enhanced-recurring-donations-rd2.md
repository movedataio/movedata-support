Enhanced Recurring Donations (RD2) is a feature in Salesforce's Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP) that provides advanced functionality for managing recurring donations. MoveData's recurring donation flows include built-in support for detecting and working with RD2.

The Recurring Mapping Flow (`[MoveData] Donation: Recurring - Mapping`) automatically detects whether Enhanced Recurring Donations is enabled in your Salesforce org and adjusts its behaviour accordingly.

For additional information, please see [Recurring Mapping Flow](https://support.movedata.io/reference/extension/npsp-fundraising/flows-recurring-donations/recurring-mapping-flow/#enhanced-recurring-donations-rd2-support_1).
