MoveData provides an ability to process or ignore offline donations, which is easily configurable in your MoveData Settings.

### Nonprofit Success Pack

{% embed url="https://app.arcade.software/share/jrqglta6OSN491MQiIq8" %}

#### Transcription

* The donation is an offline donation and no records produced
* To change this we can open Settings
* Open Fundraising
* Toggle Ignore Offline Donations to False
* Click Update to save your changes
* We can now reprocess our notification
* Observe that records are now produced for the offline donation
