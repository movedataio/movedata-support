# Security Questionnaire

## Will MoveData complete my bespoke Security Questionnaire?

We are unable to complete your bespoke security questionnaire. If you need to complete such a document, we recommend reviewing the following articles and incorporating our responses and policies into your specific document:

* [Cyber Security FAQ](https://docs.movedata.io/en/articles/9387545-cyber-security-faq)
* [Cloud Security Alliance Assessment](https://docs.movedata.io/en/articles/9812030-cloud-security-alliance-assessment)
* [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.movedata.io/legal/data-processing-addendum/)
* [Transfer Risk Assessment](https://docs.movedata.io/en/articles/9812030-cloud-security-alliance-assessment)
* [Privacy Policy](https://www.movedata.io/legal/privacy-policy/)

If you have remaining questions after doing this, please submit a support ticket or contact your account manager and we can assist with responses to any questions you cannot answer.

If you need MoveData to formally complete your blank document, we can perform this function, however this is not a standard service we offer and as such we charge a fee for the effort involved. This is covered under [Implementation Support](https://www.movedata.io/legal/movedata-support/) and typically takes between 4-8h effort depending on the nature of your document.
