# Grant Salesforce Access to MoveData

From time to time when you submit a support ticket to MoveData we may require access to your Salesforce Org. This is to review your request comprehensively and respond or resolve. In order to provide access you must do the following:

* Open Salesforce and click your avatar from the top right hand corner
* Click `Settings`
* Click `My Personal Information` → `Grant Account Login Access`
* Grant access to `MoveData.io Support` (typically for `1 Week` or `1 Month`)

Please inform MoveData you have performed the above steps, as Salesforce does not otherwise issue a notification that access has been provided.

If `MoveData.io Support` does not appear as a party to grant login access to, please reply as such on your ticket and we will suggest alternate approaches. This is only relevant to older installations of MoveData which do not have this feature.
