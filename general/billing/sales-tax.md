# Sales Tax

MoveData may or may not charge Sales Tax depending on the country in which you are consuming MoveData's services.

| Country         | Tax        | Charged | Comment                                                                        |
| --------------- | ---------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Australia       | GST @ 10%  | Yes     | GST is charged at 10% on top of your MoveData invoice                          |
| United Kingdom  | VAT @ 20%  | No      | Reverse Charge mechanism applies                                               |
| New Zealand     | GST @ 15%  | No      | Not applicable because MoveData does not exceed New Zealand threshold criteria |
| United States   | Varies     | No      | Not applicable because MoveData does not exceed US State threshold criteria    |
| The Netherlands | VAT @ 21%  | No      | Reverse Charge mechanism applies                                               |
| Switzerland     | VAT @ 8.1% | No      | Reverse Charge mechanism applies                                               |
