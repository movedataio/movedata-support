# Payment Options for your Subscription

our MoveData subscription can be paid on a monthly or annual basis.

* If you elect to pay monthly, your payment must be made via either card payment or direct debit
* If you elect to pay annually, your payment can also be made via bank transfer

The default billing frequency is monthly. Please submit a ticket if you would like to change your subscription frequency.

### Set Up Automatic Payments <a href="#h_08a8a53aac" id="h_08a8a53aac"></a>

When you receive your invoice there will be a link to view and pay online. Click the link and notice a `Set up Auto Pay` link in the top right hand side:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Auto Pay.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Automatic Payments via Credit or Debit Card <a href="#h_d35a603079" id="h_d35a603079"></a>

Enter your card details and ensure you select `Set up Auto pay and pay future invoices automatically on their due dates` before making payment.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Card Payment.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

our card information will be stored on file (via Stripe - link to [security at Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/security)) and charged when your next invoice becomes due.

#### Automatic Payments via Direct Debit <a href="#h_f693c65413" id="h_f693c65413"></a>

Click the `Direct Debit` button at the bottom of the interface which appears when you click `Set up Auto Pay`. You can then follow the prompts to set up a direct debit (via GoCardless - link to [security at GoCardless](https://gocardless.com/en-au/faq/merchants/security/)).

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Direct Debit.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Manage Automatic Payments <a href="#h_08028e674a" id="h_08028e674a"></a>

If you need to manage automatic payments, open any invoice and click the `Manage Auto Pay` button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Manage Auto Pay.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Refunds <a href="#h_8a8926190e" id="h_8a8926190e"></a>

Please review our [Pricing & Refund Policy](https://www.movedata.io/legal/pricing-refund-policy/). If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please submit a support ticket to [support@movedata.io](mailto:support@movedata.io).
