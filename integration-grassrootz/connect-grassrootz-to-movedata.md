# Connect Grassrootz to MoveData

Connecting to the Grassrootz API requires your Client Key and Secret. These are issued by Grassrootz and are not available through their Admin panel. To obtain these please email [support@grassrootz.com](mailto:support@grassrootz.com).

Once you have received your Client Key and Secret, open MoveData from the App Launcher and click Integrations → New Integration and select Grassrootz.

Follow the prompts and MoveData will generate an integration URL:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Grassrootz Integration Wizard.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Enter your Client Key and Secret:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/GR Client and Secret.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Open your Grassrootz Admin panel and click Webhooks under `Hi, [Name]` from the top right hand side:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/GR Top Right Settings.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select Create Webhook and enter the integration URL from your MoveData account and click Save:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/GR Webhook.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Grassrootz is now connected.
