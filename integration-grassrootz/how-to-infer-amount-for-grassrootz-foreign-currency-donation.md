MoveData provides the ability to infer the transaction amount for Grassrootz foreign currency donations, which is easily configurable for your Grassrootz integration.

{% embed url="https://movedata.share.arcade.software/share/rNQkQ9hrNrROcMsblggf" %}

#### Transcription

* Open Integrations
* Locate and open your Grassrootz integration
* Click Edit
* Click Next
* Togle Infer Settlement Amount if Foreign Currency to True
* Click Save
* MoveData will now infer the transaction amount for Grassrootz foreign currency donations
