Funraisin supports registration and events with multiple ticket holders. However, this information is not exposed via the Funraisin APIs and therefore, cannot be included as part of a MoveData notification.

In the above example, Jane Doe purchased two tickets - one for Jane and one for John Doe. However, the notification to MoveData will be for two tickets with Jane Doe as the purchaser.
