# Missing $0 Transactions

When a donation or purchase is executed on the Funraisin platform, it creates a transaction record. This record is used to construct a product sale notification for MoveData. However, when a product is purchased for $0, then a transaction is not created. As a result, there is no notification generated for MoveData to process.

This may change in the future and if it does, MoveData will support zero dollar notifications.
