Over time, and typically due to manually modifying your Funraisin Raffle Tickets, a scenario can occur where a contact has purchased a Funraisin Ticket Option, which is no longer present in Funraisin. This will produce a `Could not match on raffle ticket option` error.

In the following scenario, we can see Funraisin is providing the purchased ticket's Option ID as `13`

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-09 at 10.44.23 am.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

However, there are no corresponding Funraisin Raffle Tickets with the same option ID. Therefore MoveData cannot match to the Ticket Option in Funraisin as it no longer exists.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-12-09 at 10.54.06 am.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will need to contact Funraisin Support about why the raffle has a ticket option that is no longer present.
