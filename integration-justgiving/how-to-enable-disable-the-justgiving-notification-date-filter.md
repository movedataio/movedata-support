JustGiving tends to send historical information via their API. Here's how you can set a date filter to exclude that historical information from Salesforce.

{% embed url="https://app.arcade.software/share/2qlVF8fBaEwZU9UH4fzl" %}

#### Transcription

* Open MoveData from the App Launcher and click Integrations
* Open JustGiving
* Click Edit
* Scroll to Notification Date Filter and select a date. MoveData will not process donations and fundraising pages made prior to this date.
* Click Save
